// リリース時のオリジナル・テキスト
Green Waves — Chronicles (album, 2025). Dark progressive / psy tech on Padang Records, Brazil. Across ten chapters, Chronicles is a map—drawn at night, folded by the dancefloor, creased by long drives and longer afterthoughts. Each track captures a meeting, a shift, a discovery. What started as fragments—faces, places, transmissions at the edge of sleep—returns here as rhythm and tone. The grooves move like fault lines: patient, pressurized, then suddenly open, revealing rooms inside the low end where details flicker and resolve. Shaped within Padang Records’ shadow-lit terrain, Chronicles carries the Green Waves current—dark progressive with a quiet insistence, sub-bass that walks, percussion that thinks. Melodic threads surface like landmarks you recognize without recalling when you’ve been there; textures breathe in and out, trading weight for space, tension for glide. It’s music for late hours and clear minds, built to hold both the pulse of a floor and the focus of headphones. Taken together, these ten pieces read like field notes from a longer journey: coordinates sketched in kick and drone, margins annotated with small revelations. Chronicles doesn’t spell out the path so much as invite you to travel it—to feel how encounters become grooves, how transformations become tone, how the road itself becomes the record you hear.
All Tracks written and produced by Artur Vilela Master by SM Analogic Mastering UK
