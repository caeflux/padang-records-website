このリリースについて

// リリース時のオリジナル・テキスト
In the beginning, there were shadows. Flickering, shifting, silent specters, dancing on the walls of a forgotten cave. Men and women, bound in chains of their own making, worshiping the echoes of a world they could not see. Blind to the fire, blind to the light, blind to the song calling them home. But somewhere, deep within the hollow, a rhythm began to rise. A pulse. A beat. A whisper that turned to thunder. Break the Chains, it said. And some heard it. They climbed. They crawled. Hands bloodied, minds shattered, hearts wide open. They left the shadows behind. They rose toward the unknown, toward the shimmering light of a new dawn. Now, here we are—standing on the edge of the great divide. The cave behind us, the stars above us, the music inside us. Do you hear it? The electric hum, the primal throb. Machines and humans, dancing in communion. Time dissolves. Chains shatter. A symphony of freedom, born from chaos, carrying us higher. Step into the fire. Let it burn away the lies. Dance your way to truth. The shadows are gone. The light is here. But the question remains: Will you stay in the cave, or will you join us in the firelight of forever?
