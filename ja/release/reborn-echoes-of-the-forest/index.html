このリリースについて

// リリース時のオリジナル・テキスト
Between breath and memory, a quiet fracture opened— the kind that shifts the weight of a heart without asking permission. From it rose a low pulse, like the echo of a familiar step coming from a place with no body left, yet still pointing the way. Nineteen artists felt this call. Not as a message, but as a soft heaviness in the chest, a signal turning the soul back toward its true direction. They answered with sound— roots pushing through shadow, small lights cutting paths where silence once ruled. Reborn — Echoes of the Forest emerges from that movement: dark progressive without ornament, shaped by those who know the night teaches, and that some presences remain long after they depart. Distinct voices tuned by the same awakening— the kind that comes when loss is faced and life insists on continuing. Here, the forest breathes deeply. It holds the footsteps that came before, guides the ones that follow, and turns absence into direction. Each track carries a spark of this rebirth, each artist moves the darkness a little closer to life. Step in. The forest is awake. And its heart remembers.
Compiled by Vagner Donada (aka Tekmall) Dedicated to the memory of Leandro Donada, his brother Mastering by SM Analogic Mastering UK Artwork by Fernando Fetex
