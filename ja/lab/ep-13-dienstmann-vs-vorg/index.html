// soundcloud のエピソード原文
Join Dienstmann and Vorg as they take the stage together for the first time in a dynamic tribute to Padang Records' 11th anniversary. This electrifying performance features an unforgettable lineup of classic tracks from the label’s storied history, alongside exclusive unreleased material soon to debut on the label. Don’t miss this journey through the evolution of dark progressive and psy tech!
