// texto original del lanzamiento
MinimalTech Series Vol. 1, 'What Creates Illusion,' is a transformative V.A. compiled by Nidhi Singh, also known as Black 11. Hailing from India, Nidhi a multidisciplinary force in the nation's underground scene for over a decade. This compilation marks a pinnacle of artistic exploration. It features thirteen alchemical tracks engineered not merely to challenge perception, but to rewire the listener's neural pathways. Drawing from Goa's rich psychedelic heritage while venturing into the uncharted sonic territories of the Thar Desert, each composition is a meditation on rhythm, space, and the very fabric of existence. This is an inward journey, an invitation to explore the depths of consciousness and discover the profound connection between sound and self. Prepare for an auditory adventure that transcends the ordinary to reveal the extraordinary.
Compiled by Black11 Artwork by Nidhi Singh and Flying Eyes Master by SM Mastering UK
