Nyctophilia And a seeker stood before the night and asked: “What is this longing I feel when the sun bows to the horizon? Why does my soul stir when the world is cloaked in darkness?” And the night spoke, saying: "You are not lost in the dark, but found. For in the absence of light, your spirit is no longer bound by the illusions of the day. Here, in the stillness, you remember. You become." "This music is not of the world you know, nor is it of the silence beyond. It is the voice of the unseen, the rhythm of shadows stretching long, the whisper of the wind that knows no master. It moves like the tide, not to drown you, but to cleanse. It trembles like fire, not to burn, but to illuminate. It calls you forth, not as a prisoner, but as a dancer upon the threshold of all things hidden." "Do not fear the night, for it is only another face of the beloved. And do not fear the sound, for it is but your own heartbeat echoed back to you. Step forth into the darkness, for here, you will find not an end, but a beginning."
