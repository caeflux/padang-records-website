Various Artists — Dusk Till Dawn (VA, 2021). 20-track dark progressive / psy tech compilation on Padang Records, Brazil. The 3'rd assortment of long waited compilation that took a year to collab, without any dispensations on quality nor diversity, is finally here this time with the help of the collab between 2 exceptional labels Jaira/Padang Each one bring that extra glimmer by the most instigated, reinventing artists that transport their witchcraft within the creation of that magical time zone between light/dark and the twilight between.
