// bio

A striking presence in the Portuguese underground since 2012, expanding beyond borders in the years that followed — her art took her to some of the world's biggest festivals: Burning Man, Modem Festival (Croatia), Boom Festival (Portugal), Esoteric Festival, Fora de Tempo Festival and GemFest. Signed to Padang Records in 2018 and curated TechnoMad — the label's most successful compilation to date — and headlined Padang Lab Series EP.11. After crossing multiple continents, planes and stories, she now arrives with something raw, focused and uncompromising. Maintains the experimental alter-ego Inthelinbo (evolved from experiences in the depths of the Australian bush — fast rhythms meeting ethereal atmospheres) and the more intimate side Deepinthelin, where the beat breaks, the subconscious twists and reflection is demanded.
