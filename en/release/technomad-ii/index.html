// original release notes
The eagerly awaited Volume 2 of one of Padang Records’ most distinctive VA’s comes to life through the vision of Anaïs Lin, a powerful presence in underground electronic scene. The result of over a year of profound research and meticulous curation, this release reveals a alchemy merging hypnotic tech, tribal percussions, and ritualistic atmospheres—a musical narrative designed to lead mind and body along hypnotic, transcendent paths. Each track was carefully analog-mastered at the renowned SM Studios UK, ensuring warmth, depth, and authenticity.
