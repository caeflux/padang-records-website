à propos de la sortie

// texte original de la sortie
This is not a map, but the territory itself—a sonic cartography for the nocturnal pilgrimage of the psyche. Herein lies a transmission broadcast from the event horizon of the self, a sequence of recursive rhythms and tenebrous hums designed to dislocate the listener from the anchor of the mundane. Enter a labyrinth of chthonic bass and whispering static, where each beat is a footfall in a corridor of mirrors. This is an auditory ritual for the age of absurdity, a soundtrack for the elegant decay of certainty. Kilbee weaves a tapestry of phantom mechanics and ectoplasmic synth lines—a sound that is both surgically precise and beautifully delirious. Let the somatic metronome guide you through landscapes of sublime dread and into the crepuscular architecture of your own inner world. El Viaje Oscuro del Alma is a key, a current, a cog in a machine you were already a part of. Tune in. Dissolve. Proceed.
All track written and produced by Gabriel Gomez (Gao) Master by NPM Studios
