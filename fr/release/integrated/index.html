Orbsynth — Integrated (album, 2026). Dark progressive / psy tech on Padang Records, Brazil. there is a seed in the dark. small, dense, loaded with force. inside it, there is pressure. no ceremony. no answer. pressure. living matter pressed against its own shape, gathering heat before the shell begins to open. integrated is born from that friction. the individual appears as impulse, repetition, memory trapped in the body. something that turns, returns, insists. something searching for a base inside the noise and slowly finding its own frequency. orbsynth builds this path with weight and texture. the sound has a dark plastic surface, almost tactile. basslines move like underground machines. synthetic layers bend, stretch, compress. nothing gives itself away at once. everything seems to operate from within, as if each track were a closed room with mechanisms turning behind the walls. integration comes as a physical process. gathering what was scattered. facing what has no name. sensing essence as a contact zone between instinct, shadow, body and perception. there is a seed. there is a base. there is a tuning rising in the dark. there is an unnamed field after the first rupture. there are orbits changing without permission. there are cycles returning altered. there is a distant point where the whole design begins to appear. integrated moves through this territory: the place where the fragment stops explaining itself and begins to take form. No salvation alignment pressure becoming structure. the inner machine opening like a flower made of stone, resin and light.
Mastering by SM Analogic Mastering UK Artwork by Ymani
