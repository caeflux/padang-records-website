// texte original de la sortie
Various Artists — 2025 Anthology (free VA, 2025). 30 tracks of dark progressive / psy tech from the Padang Records roster, Brazil. Name your price. They say the dance never ends. Barong moves first — teeth, rhythm, protection. Rangda moves after — the truth we avoid, the shadow that waits. We spend our lives building temples of “good,” painting our masks brighter, hoping the darkness won’t recognize us. But the darkness is the part of us that connects us to everything. And when we finally turn to face it, it doesn’t destroy — it completes. Here, the dance becomes a mirror. A conversation. A sacred argument. A spiral toward wholeness. 2025 Anthology is offered freely — a small blessing, a thank you, an offering of smoke left at the gate. For everyone who has listened, shared, danced, and kept Padang alive in the invisible places. Take it. Keep it. Pass it on. May it find you in the hour you need it. And when the fog rises, may you recognize yourself — fully, finally — in both the smile and the teeth. — Dienstmann Padang Records
