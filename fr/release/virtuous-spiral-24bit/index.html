à propos de la sortie

// texte original de la sortie
In the echoing abyss of the sonic universe, where rhythm becomes the heartbeat of the spectral void, a new auditory journey spirals out from the heart of the Patagonian wilderness. Chilean artist Bensaid forges this voyage in the form of "Virtuous Spiral," an EP birthed in an unusual studio sanctuary, nestled amongst the ancient whispering trees. Every pulsating beat within "Virtuous Spiral" echoes the symbiosis of the artist and his elemental surroundings, an intimate dialogue between creation and creator. The EP reverberates with a raw energy that dances on the feral edge of the techno spectrum, entwining the haunting melancholy of the forest’s voice in each track. Yet, "Virtuous Spiral" transcends the boundaries of an auditory experience. It's an enigmatic invocation of the cosmos, of chaos and order, and of the primal dance of existence. Unfurling like the spiraling arms of a galaxy, the EP draws the listener into its hypnotic orbit, inviting them to traverse its labyrinth of sound, and to discover within its depths the profound, unspoken poetry of existence.
All tracks written and produced by Benjamin Garrido Master by NPM Artwork by Ymani Design
