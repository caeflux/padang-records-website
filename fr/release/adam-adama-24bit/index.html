à propos de la sortie

// texte original de la sortie
Qawa Beat, a musical mystic with 25 years of experience and a legacy as the founder of Jaira Records, crafts this celestial journey, weaving the very fabric of existence into sound. Released under Padang Records, "Adam Adama" is an odyssey through time and space, connecting the earthly with the galactic. A cosmic reflection of ourselves, this album explores the boundaries of Psychedelic, Jazz, Minimal, and Deep Tech, resonating with the heartbeat of the universe. It's an exploration of what it means to be human, a meditation on our place within the infinite, and a celebration of our profound connection to the natural world. Each note is a step on a path that leads back to the source, back to the earth from which we came. Each melody is a ripple in the cosmic pond, a call to the ancient and the eternal. "Adam Adama" invites you to dance with the unknown, to lose yourself in the labyrinth of sound, and to find yourself in the silence that follows. It's a journey to the core of existence, where music becomes a living force, a guide, a reflection of our primal nature. In the hands of Qawa Beat, "Adam Adama" transcends music; it becomes a mirror to our souls, a bridge between the seen and the unseen, a sacred communion with the essence of life. Join the odyssey. Embrace the mystery. Connect with the earth, with the stars, with yourself.
All tracks written and produced by Eliran Ohayon Master by QBP Mastering Artwork by Ymani
